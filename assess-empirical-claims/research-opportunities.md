# From criticism to a paper

Treat each material criticism as a candidate contribution. The task is to identify what could be learned and how, not to guarantee that every objection deserves a separate publication. Work within the authorized scope; proposing data collection does not authorize recruiting people, spending money, or contacting authors.

## Make the opportunity concrete

For each finding, record a short row or paragraph with:

- **Established fact:** the reproduced result, confirmed defect, or documented access limit, with its source and uncertainty.
- **Open question:** what the existing design cannot distinguish.
- **Contribution:** a correction, improved measurement, mechanism test, scope-condition result, methodological demonstration, new dataset, or independent replication.
- **Decisive evidence:** the comparison or intervention that separates the original interpretation from its strongest rival, including a result that would support the original paper.
- **Data and design:** what is already available, what must be collected, the unit/population/estimand, and identifying assumptions.
- **Feasibility and order:** the cheapest informative next analysis, expected access and computation constraints, and what would make the project not worth pursuing.
- **Writing claim:** the strongest sentence supported now and the stronger sentence that would require new evidence. Never write proposed results as findings.

Prioritize by interpretive importance, discriminating power, feasibility, and potential contribution. Avoid a numerical scoring system unless it actually improves a decision. Verify novelty against the literature when access permits; otherwise label novelty unassessed.

## Route to the user's other skills

- Use `empirical-problem-solving` when there is a concrete discrepancy with an unknown cause. Preserve failed hypotheses and minimize the example.
- Use `build-data` when the proposed contribution needs new joins, recodes, or undocumented data.
- Use `design-analysis` when choosing a genuinely new empirical design. State which results have already been seen; do not retroactively call an exploratory reanalysis preregistered. Freeze future choices before collecting or examining new outcome evidence where feasible.
- Use `audit-analysis` to check the resulting analysis and claim-to-code agreement.
- Use `write-empirical-paper` for the manuscript and repository, with `on-writing` for the argument and `visualize-evidence` for exhibits. Keep generated numbers synchronized across prose, tables, figures, and README. Follow the existing user's authorization rather than adding redundant approval gates.

## Examples of opportunities, not completed studies

**Water markets:** Village dominance may misdescribe a farmer's feasible suppliers. Map fields and wells across administrative boundaries, measure distance, prices, delivery reliability, and seller caste, and test whether the gap shrinks with genuinely available alternatives. Boundary proximity by itself is not random assignment; a design exploiting new access needs its own assumptions. A result showing no caste difference conditional on actual opportunities would support a geographic explanation; persistent transaction differences could support a caste mechanism.

**Marriage choices:** The same-caste coefficient is not a universal price of avoiding lower castes. Reconstruct full contrasts and identify who made the decision. A new study could vary partner attributes or information and separately record relatives' and prospective spouses' choices. It would need to distinguish preferences from expected acceptance and stated intentions from consequential behavior. The existing regressions alone do not make that experiment's result known.

**IV:** An exclusion concern can strengthen rather than weaken an estimate when its direction is considered. A methods or replication note could systematically compare plausible pathways, empirical magnitude benchmarks, and sensitivity regions. Specify the direct-effect assumptions and where their calibration comes from; an arbitrarily selected harmful violation is not a contribution.
